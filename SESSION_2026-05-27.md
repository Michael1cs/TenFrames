### Voice doesn't follow you out of FreePlay
**Symptom:** child solves a Free Play subtraction problem, then taps
into Adventure during the 5-second confetti window. Five seconds
later, the FreePlay `setTimeout` in `useGameState.handleSubmit` fires
`doGenerateProblem()`, the per-problem voice `useEffect` in
`GameShell` wakes up because `currentProblem` changed, and the next
problem's narration plays over the Adventure screen.

**Fix:**
- `useShellState` now exposes a `freePlayFocusedRef` ref.
- `FreePlayScreen` (the stack route) flips it via `useFocusEffect`:
  true on focus, false on blur.
- The per-problem voice `useEffect` in `GameShell` bails early when
  the ref is false. The `setTimeout` still runs and generates the
  next problem internally, but the narration is suppressed because
  the screen isn't on screen.
- Note: react-navigation's blur listener already calls
  `voice.stop()` at the moment of navigation. The new ref blocks
  the LATER setTimeout-driven narration that wasn't covered by the
  immediate stop.
